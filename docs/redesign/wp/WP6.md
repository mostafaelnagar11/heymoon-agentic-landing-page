## Requests to the lead

1. **Horizon (WP0, `globals.css`).** (a) is done: globals.css carries the sky below the apex and the transparent ground, and WP6's duplicate overrides are deleted (fix round). Still open, (b): `.hz-rim`, `.hz-earth`, `.hz-hair` heights `max(200px, calc(12.1vw + 64px))` (the limb is 12.02vw below the apex at the edges; the 200px layers cut it from about 1630px wide). The exact CSS is in `close.module.css` under `.hzCell`; when globals adopts it, delete those two lines there.
2. **Morph timing (globals `.morph`, hero H1 and close H2).** With `hl-in` starting at 120ms on `--ease-out-expo` and `hl-out` on `--ease-exit` (an ease-in), the two lines overlap in the same line box from about 200 to 400ms: the new line is ~70% risen while the old one has moved ~20% (frames: `shots/WP6/morph2-grid.png`). It reads as a double exposure in stills. Options: start `in` at ~280ms, or run `out` on `--ease-out`. I left it, since it is shared with WP1.
3. **Anchor restore on the live page (WP0 `lib/scroll.ts`, LEAD-B this round).** Deterministic, not "packages mid-edit": `restoreAnchor` realigns once and re-checks one frame later, but `content-visibility: auto` sections above the close render for the new audience **after** that, at a height that differs from their size hint, so the close (and the switch under the cursor) jumps by the difference. Verifier: brands → creators with the close top at 300.0 ends at 329.9 (the number section 1086 → 1116 about 200ms after the commit, scrollY unchanged); creators → brands 300.4 → 269.5. Fix round re-run (`wp6fix/fork2.js`, 1440x900, close top 300.1): ends at 549, because now the agents section also grows after the restore (528 → 750) on top of the number (1080 → 1107). Offsets 0 to -150 hold (the change is all above the viewport top only when the close is high). The lab, which has no cv sections, holds within 0.1px at 300/120/0/-60. Fix (the verifier's): after the first realign keep realigning for a short window, e.g. `const ro = new ResizeObserver(() => { lenis?.resize(); realign(a); })` observing every `[data-slot]`, disconnected after 1000ms or on the first wheel, touchstart or keydown (user input wins); or listen for `contentvisibilityautostatechange` over the same window. Nothing in WP6's files can fix this: the close's own path is correct. Testing note: Playwright's `page.click` scrolls the target into view first, which moves the close by itself; dispatch the click from `evaluate` when probing this.
4. **RTL (WP0 Field).** (LEAD-A this round.) With `dir="rtl"` on `<html>`, the field's typed hint sits under the Start button (`shots/WP6/rtl-bottom-1440.png`): the hint is positioned with logical `start-0 ps-[52px]` while the input is `dir="ltr"`. Not WP6's file.
5. **Nav compact switch at the close (WP0 `shell/Nav.tsx`, `lib/signals.ts`).** At the bottom of the page the nav's compact switch and the close's switch are both on screen, 125px apart at 1440x900 and 115px on phone: two identical controls. Suggested: a `closeSwitchVisible` signal like `heroSwitchVisible`, written by `AudienceSwitch placement="close"`, and the nav hides its switch while it is true. Base it on an IntersectionObserver with `rootMargin: "-88px 0px 0px 0px"` (phone `-80px`), so it matches WP6's tuck (deviation 13): the moment the close switch starts tucking under the pill, the nav's switch comes back, and there is never a moment with neither. No JS: the nav has no switch and the close switch tucks at max scroll in short windows (1280x720), so the bottom screen has only the footer's Brands/Creators links there; scrolling up a little shows the close switch again.
6. **Nav surface over the sheet's rounded bottom (WP0 `shell/Nav.tsx`).** For the 32px of scroll while the sheet's rounded bottom passes the nav's centre line, the nav picks the close (later in DOM order) and uses the night skin over paper: a muddy grey pill with white text (verifier `shots/WP6v/tour-brands-1440-close.png`). Suggested: prefer the surface that paints on top at the centre line, e.g. the first `[data-surface]` in `document.elementsFromPoint(innerWidth / 2, navTop + navH / 2)`. The -32px overlap is §5.6's `-mt-8`, so WP6 keeps it.
