### Requests to the lead (fix round)

5. **LEAD-A, `app/(site)/globals.css` line 94:** make the shared rule `.draw { stroke-dasharray: 1 2; stroke-dashoffset: 1; }` (it is `1` today). With `1` the pattern puts a zero-length dash exactly on the path's end, and a round cap paints it as a dot on 2x screens before the line draws. The WP8 mocks no longer depend on this: they set the dash inline (`drawStroke` in `mocks/parts.tsx`, with a 1.02 undrawn offset as margin). But any future `.draw` user would inherit the dot. The change alters nothing at offset 0 or mid-draw, and the reduced-motion rule needs no change.
6. **WP3, for the record:** MockTerms and MockCheck now fit WP3's tall mounts whole at 360 to 1440 (measured cards 359 and 325 to 349px). MockRead clips inside its own card in any mount shorter than its list (see item 10), so `.tallRead` can stay at 440. WP3's note that "MockCheck's card stays under its 360px compaction query" still holds: no WP8 container-query threshold changed.
